Meet Thube

Hi, I'm Thube, a qualified special needs teacher with an Honours in Psychology. As a mother of an autistic child myself, I understand the unique challenges and beautiful strengths that come with learning differences. I work at Creative Kidz in Alberton, Brackendowns, where I've dedicated my career to supporting children and families on their learning journey. My approach combines professional expertise with genuine compassion and personal understanding.
